# Timeline / Chronology (compilation)

Shallow scope/audience/format tree — see SKILL.md Step 4, compilation mode.

## Questions

- Scope — the full history of the setting, or one era/arc's worth?
- Does the setting have a Calendar note establishing how dates are expressed? Fact-find first and use its convention rather than defaulting to real-world date formatting.
- Should this include only major Event notes, or also smaller dated occurrences pulled from other notes' History sections?

## Synthesis approach

Compile chronologically from Event notes plus any dated details found in other notes' History/founding-date fields. Flag date conflicts found during compilation (two notes implying contradictory timing) rather than silently picking one.
