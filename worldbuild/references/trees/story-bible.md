# Story Bible (compilation)

Shallow scope/audience/format tree. See SKILL.md Step 4, compilation mode. Unlike entity categories, it has no Infobox/Document-section split. The shape of the output depends on what synthesis finds, not on a fixed template.

## Questions (asked as one round, not deep branching)

- Who is this for? A co-writer, an editor, a future version of yourself, or an artist/illustrator who needs setting reference? The answer sets depth and jargon level more than anything else.
- Scope: the whole world, or one book/arc's worth of material? A story bible for book two of a trilogy should not list every location in the wider world, only what is relevant to what is being written now.
- Format preference: a single long document, or split by category (characters.md, locations.md, etc.)? Fact-find the notes' existing organization first and match it instead of imposing a new structure.

## Synthesis approach

Once scope is settled, read across the categories the scope implies, and compile instead of re-interviewing. This is not a grilling session about new content. It assembles what already exists into one reference document. Flag gaps found along the way, such as a character mentioned in three places with no note of their own, instead of silently working around them.
