# Story Bible (compilation)

Shallow scope/audience/format tree — see SKILL.md Step 4, compilation mode. No Infobox/Document-section split the way entity categories have; the shape of the output depends on what synthesis actually finds, not a fixed template.

## Questions (asked as one round, not deep branching)

- Who is this for — a co-writer, an editor, a future version of yourself, an artist/illustrator who needs setting reference? This determines depth and jargon level more than anything else.
- Scope — the whole world, or one book/arc's worth of relevant material? A story bible for book two of a trilogy shouldn't necessarily surface every location in the wider world, only what's relevant to what's being written now.
- Format preference — a single long document, or split by category (characters.md, locations.md, etc.)? Fact-find the notes' existing organization first; match it rather than imposing a new structure.

## Synthesis approach

Once scope is settled, read broadly across the categories the scope implies, and compile rather than re-interview — this is not a grilling session about new content, it's assembling what already exists into one reference-friendly shape. Flag gaps found along the way (a character mentioned in three places with no actual note of their own) rather than silently working around them.
