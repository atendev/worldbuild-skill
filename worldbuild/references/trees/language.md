# Language / Script

WA source: `language.html.twig`.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus the following. **Alphabet/script**, or a note that the language is unwritten. **Geographic distribution**. **Speaker ethnicities/cultures**, as a relation, fact-find first.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Phonology & Phonetics**, meaning what the language sounds like. Even a rough description helps a writer stay consistent when rendering dialogue or names. **Morphology & Syntax**, only as deep as a novel needs, since most fiction needs flavor and not a full grammar. **Naming Conventions**, meaning male, female, and unisex name patterns. If this overlaps heavily with an associated Culture note's own Naming Conventions section, link to it rather than duplicate it. **Vocabulary Highlights**, a handful of notable words and concepts that do not translate cleanly and can be used in dialogue. **Common Phrases**. **History & Spread**.
