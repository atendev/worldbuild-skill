# Creature / Monster

No dedicated WA source. This reuses Species' non-sapient branch at a narrower scale, often for an individual of note, such as a specific named monster or a distinct creature type too minor to warrant a full Species entry.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Classification**, a relation to a Species note if this creature belongs to a broader documented species; **Habitat**; **Diet**; **Danger level**, a short descriptor or game-relevant rating; **Size**.

## Root decision: is this a named individual or a creature type?

- **Individual.** A specific, singular notable creature, such as a named dragon or a particular haunting spirit. Treat it much like a lightweight Character. It can have a personality, a history, and a role in the plot.
- **Type.** A category of creature that appears repeatedly without individual identity, such as a species of wolf-analogue or a recurring monster-of-the-week. Focus on the general pattern, not a specific instance.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Physical Description**, **Behavior & Temperament**, **Habitat & Ecology**, **Abilities & Attacks**, especially useful for game-adjacent settings; **Notable Individuals**, only if Type was chosen above and specific standout examples exist, linking out rather than duplicating if they are substantial enough for their own note; **Lore & Legends**, linking to Myth notes rather than restating them here.
