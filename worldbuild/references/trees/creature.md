# Creature / Monster

No dedicated WA source — this reuses Species' non-sapient branch at a narrower, often individually-notable scale (a specific named monster, or a distinct creature-type too minor to warrant a full Species entry).

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Classification** (relation to a Species note, if this creature belongs to a broader documented species), **Habitat**, **Diet**, **Danger level** (a short descriptor or game-relevant rating), **Size**.

## Root decision — is this a named individual or a creature-type?

- **Individual** — a specific, singular notable creature (a named dragon, a particular haunting spirit). Treat much like a lightweight Character: it can have a personality, a history, a role in the plot.
- **Type** — a category of creature that appears repeatedly without individual identity (a species of wolf-analogue, a recurring monster-of-the-week). Focus on the general pattern, not a specific instance.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Physical Description**, **Behavior & Temperament**, **Habitat & Ecology**, **Abilities & Attacks** (especially useful for game-adjacent settings), **Notable Individuals** (only if Type was chosen above and specific standout examples exist — link out rather than duplicate if they're substantial enough for their own note), **Lore & Legends** (link to Myth notes rather than restating them here).
