# Origin / Background (D&D 5e)

WA source: None. Built from the structure of the user's existing homebrew origins. D&D 5e only, using the 2024 background shape.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name plus: **Epigraph** (a short in-world quote, if the user's origins use one); **Ability scores** (three to choose from); **Skill proficiencies** (two); **Tool proficiency** (one); **Equipment** (with its total gold value. 2024 backgrounds offer the kit or 50 gp); **Origin feat** (an existing feat, or a new one); **Lineage group** (if this origin belongs to a set, such as several heritages of one bloodline, a relation to the group's note).

## Document sections (prose body)

**Flavor intro** (one or two paragraphs, second person, matching the user's existing origins).

**In the world** (where people with this origin come from, who they owe, and how others treat them. Fact-find the related Location, Faction, or Culture notes first).

**Origin feat** (if it is new: its benefits, written as named bullets. 2024 origin feats are weaker than general feats, so compare against the official ones before the confirmation gate).

**System hooks** (only if the origin connects to a Magic system with its own rules. Ask how the feat touches those rules).

**Roleplay hooks** (a few prompts for personality, bonds, or flaws, if the user wants them).
