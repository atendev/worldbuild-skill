# Vehicle / Ship / Mount

WA source: `vehicle.html.twig` — heavily skewed toward sci-fi/naval vessels (armament, hangar, sensors); treat those fields as optional rather than universal for a setting where "vehicle" means a horse-drawn cart or a riding beast.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type**, **Manufacturer** (relation), **Owner** (relation to Character or Faction), **Price/Rarity**, **Dimensions** (length/beam/height as relevant), **Weight**, **Speed**, **Crew complement**, **Cargo capacity**, **Nickname/Motto** (if the vehicle itself has an identity worth naming beyond its model/type).

## Document sections (prose body)

**Propulsion & Power** (how it moves — mundane, magical, mechanical), **Armament & Defenses** (only if combat-relevant to the setting), **Systems** (sensors, communication, or setting-equivalent — skip entirely for low-tech vehicles), **Hangar/Cargo Capacity** (what it carries, if notable), **Datasheet** (only if the setting uses mechanical vehicle stats), **History** (notable voyages, battles, previous owners).
