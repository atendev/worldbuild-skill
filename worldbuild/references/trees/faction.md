# Faction / Organization

## Infobox fields

Asked as an early pass, before the narrative branches below. See `_shared.md` → "How Infobox fields work" for general handling, and "Shared infobox fields" for the Name/Status fields every category includes.

Faction-specific fields, in addition to the shared ones:

- **Type**. From the root decision below. Always included once that is resolved.
- **Founding date, and dissolution date if applicable**. When it formed and when, if ever, it ended, in whatever calendar or relative-time convention the setting uses. Render them together as a lifespan if both exist. If the founding date is unknown or lost to history, note that, for example "origin disputed", rather than leaving a blank.
- **Headquarters / capital / seat of power**. A specific Location if one exists in the notes (fact-find first), or a general description if it is not yet its own entry. For a Government/State type with a distinct capital city, use "capital" as the label.
- **Leader**. Condensed to a name or title here. The how and why belong in the narrative body. For a Government/State type where the symbolic and operational heads of power are different roles, such as a monarch and a prime minister, or a pope and a curial administrator, split this into **Head of state** and **Head of government**. Do this only if the user's setting draws that distinction. Do not impose the split where one person holds both roles.
- **Government system** (Government/State type only). A short phrase such as constitutional monarchy, military junta, or elected council. The fuller explanation belongs in the Government System document section.
- **Scale**. A rough size or reach descriptor, such as a dozen members in one city or a continent-spanning order, not a precise headcount unless the user has one.
- **Demonym** (Government/State and Culture-bearing factions only, meaning a nation's people and not a small guild). What a member or citizen is called.
- **Symbol** and **Motto**. Keep these separate. Symbol is the visual identifier, such as a sigil, a flag, or a color. Motto is a spoken or written phrase. Either or both may be worth one line, since small details like these get reused across scenes.

## Root decision: type gate

What kind of organization is this? Nothing below can be asked until this is settled, since the branches diverge substantially.

- Government / state. Holds formal political authority over a territory or people.
- Guild / professional body. Organized around a trade, skill, or profession.
- Religious organization. The institutional and hierarchical arm of a belief system. The belief system itself is the separate Religion category, so fact-find whether a matching Religion note exists and link to it rather than duplicate doctrine here.
- Criminal / underground. Operates outside or against established law.
- Military / martial order. Organized around armed force, combat training, or a warrior code.
- Mercantile / commercial. Organized around trade, production, or commerce.
- Social / fraternal / secret society. Bound by shared identity, belief, or oath rather than any of the above.

If none of these fit, or the faction spans more than one, such as a mercantile house that has become a shadow government or a martial order that is also a religious one, don't force a single gate. Note the blend and carry forward whichever branches below are relevant to each aspect.

## Cross-type branches (ask regardless of which gate above was taken)

- What is the faction's stated goal or purpose, and does it have a different true or hidden goal, known only to some members or to none?
- How is leadership structured, and how does power transfer? It might pass by inheritance, election, combat, seniority, divine mandate, or force. If the faction has multiple named ranks or titles worth distinguishing, don't describe the hierarchy in the abstract. For each rank worth naming, capture what it takes to hold it (qualifications and requirements), how someone is appointed or promoted into it, its duties and any perks, and what would get someone removed from it. Keep this compact per rank, a few lines each and not a full separate interview, unless one rank is central enough to the narrative to warrant real depth. These get written into the faction's own Ranks & Titles section (see Document sections below) rather than spun off as separate notes.
- How does someone join? It might be open membership, invitation, birthright, trial, or purchase. What would get someone expelled, or killed for leaving?
- What gives this faction its power or leverage? It might be wealth, numbers, military force, information or blackmail, magic, political legitimacy, or popular support.
- Is the faction internally unified, or does it have real fault lines, such as rival leadership claims, factions within the faction, or an old guard versus reformers? This affects how far "the faction" can act with one voice on the page.
- Who are its foreign relations and rivals? That includes allies, enemies, neutral parties, and anyone it borders or competes with. Fact-find first. This overlaps with the Connections fragment, but it is substantial enough to warrant its own document section rather than staying a one-line connection.

## Government / state branch

- What system of governance does it use? It might be monarchy, republic, oligarchy, theocracy, council, or something without a clean real-world analogue.
- What territory or population does it control, as opposed to claim to control?
- What makes its authority legitimate in the eyes of those it governs? It might be tradition, force, divine sanction, consent, or a mix. Is that legitimacy currently secure or contested?
- Is there a distinct legislative body that makes law and a judicial body that interprets and enforces it, or does the same authority hold both roles?

This branch also pulls in the shared Economic & Trade block below, since these questions almost always apply to a state.

## Guild / professional branch

- What trade, skill, or profession is it organized around?
- Does it hold a monopoly or licensing power over that trade, and how is that enforced?
- How do training and advancement work? It might use apprenticeship, ranks, or examinations.

## Religious organization branch

- What Religion or Deity does it serve or represent, if that is already established in the notes? Fact-find first. If no matching Religion note exists yet, flag that as a likely follow-up rather than inventing doctrine here.
- What is its role in belief and practice? Does it enforce orthodoxy, only help organize worship, or do something looser?
- Is it the sole institutional voice of its faith, or one of several competing bodies, such as sects or denominations, claiming the same religious authority?
- Does membership or clergy status grant real, mechanical power in the setting, such as blessings, magic, or authority recognized by law? Or is its power only social and institutional?

This branch's document sections run considerably deeper than the others (see Document sections below), since religious organizations tend to need more kinds of detail than any other faction type: doctrine, cosmology, practice, and hierarchy.

## Criminal / underground branch

- What illicit activity or activities does it carry out?
- How does it evade or coexist with legitimate authority? It might use bribery, secrecy, or being too useful to eliminate, or operate somewhere authority does not reach.
- Does it claim or contest territory, and against whom?

## Military / martial order branch

- Who or what does it serve? It might serve a state, a religion, itself, or a code independent of any of those.
- Is there a formal code of conduct or oath, and what happens when it is broken?
- Does it specialize in a particular combat style, terrain, or function, or operate as a general-purpose force?

## Mercantile / commercial branch

- What does it trade, produce, or control the flow of?
- Does it face real competition, or does it hold something close to a monopoly?
- How does it use the wealth it generates? It might reinvest, buy political influence, spend on luxury, or fund something else entirely.

This branch also pulls in the shared Economic & Trade block below.

## Social / fraternal / secret society branch

- How secret is it? It might be unknown to exist, known to exist with secret membership, or open but exclusive.
- What binds members beyond the stated purpose? It might be shared class, a shared secret, a blood oath, or a shared belief that does not rise to a full Religion.
- What is the consequence of secrecy being broken, and who enforces it?

## Shared Economic & Trade block (Government/State and Mercantile/Commercial types only)

- What is the economic system? It might be market, planned, guild-controlled, barter, or something else.
- What currency is used, if the setting has one distinct to this faction?
- What are its major exports and imports?
- Is agriculture and industry or trade and transport more central to how it sustains itself?

## Document sections (prose body)

Cross-type, used by every faction regardless of gate: **Structure** (how the hierarchy works day to day). **Ranks & Titles**, only if the faction has named positions worth distinguishing. Use one subsection per rank, each covering qualifications, appointment or promotion, duties, benefits, and grounds for removal, plus any notable current or past holders linked to Character notes. Keep it compact rather than treating each rank as its own full entity. **Public Agenda** (the stated purpose; private or hidden goals belong in prose too, but flag them clearly as not public knowledge). **Public Perception**. WA's fields cover only how the faction presents itself and not whether anyone believes it. Does the general population, meaning non-members, see this faction as beloved, feared, distrusted, unknown, or something that varies by region or class? Ask this explicitly, since Public Agenda and Public Perception can diverge sharply and that gap is often useful to the story. **Assets**, **History**, **Foreign Relations**, **Notable Members** (link to Character notes rather than duplicating their detail here). Include **Disbandment** only if Status marks this faction as historical or dissolved.

Government/State additional: **Demographics & Population**, **Controlled Territories**, **Government System**, **Power Structure**, **Legislative Body**, **Judicial Body**, **Military**, **Official Languages**, **Neighboring Nations**, the shared **Economic System / Currency / Exports & Imports / Agriculture & Industry / Trade & Transport** sections, and optionally **Education** and **Infrastructure** if relevant to the story.

Religious organization additional. This is the deepest section list of any type, so use what is relevant rather than all of it: **Mythology & Lore**, **Divine Origins**, **Cosmological Views**, **Tenets of Faith**, **Ethics**, **Worship Practices**, **Priesthood** (clergy structure), **Granted Divine Powers** (only if the setting has gods who mechanically grant real power), **Sects** (internal denominational splits), **Political Influence & Intrigue**.

Guild additional: **Trade & Licensing**.

Criminal additional: **Operations & Territory**.

Military/martial additional: **Code & Specialization**.

Mercantile additional: uses the Government/State Economic block above rather than a separate version.

Social/secret additional: **Secrecy & Bonds**.

## Then append `_shared.md`

Narrative function, connections to existing material, and tone consistency, in that order, after the relevant branches above are resolved.
