# Faction / Organization

## Infobox fields

Asked as an early pass, before the narrative branches below. See `_shared.md` → "How Infobox fields work" for general handling, and "Shared infobox fields" for the Name/Status fields every category includes.

Faction-specific, in addition to the shared ones:

- **Type** — from the root decision below. Always included once that's resolved.
- **Founding date, and dissolution date if applicable** — when it formed, and when (if ever) it ended, in whatever calendar/relative-time convention the setting uses. Render together as a lifespan if both exist. Skip founding date if genuinely unknown or lost to history — that can itself be a note ("origin disputed") rather than a blank.
- **Headquarters / capital / seat of power** — a specific Location if one exists in the notes (fact-find first) or a general description if not yet fleshed out as its own entry. For a Government/State type with a distinct capital city, prefer "capital" as the label.
- **Leader** — condensed to a name/title here; the how-and-why belongs in the narrative body. For a Government/State type where the symbolic and operational heads of power are genuinely different roles (a monarch versus a prime minister, a pope versus a curial administrator), split into **Head of state** and **Head of government** instead of one combined Leader field — but only if the user's setting actually draws that distinction; don't impose the split where one person holds both roles.
- **Government system** (Government/State type only) — a short phrase (constitutional monarchy, military junta, elected council) — the fuller explanation belongs in the Government System document section.
- **Scale** — rough size/reach descriptor (a dozen members in one city, a continent-spanning order) rather than requiring a precise headcount unless the user has one.
- **Demonym** (Government/State and Culture-bearing factions only — a nation's people, not a small guild) — what a member/citizen is called.
- **Symbol** and **Motto** — kept separate rather than merged into one field: Symbol is the visual identifier (a sigil, a flag, a color), Motto is a spoken/written phrase, if either exists. Either or both may be worth even one line — small details like these get reused across scenes.

## Root decision — type gate

What kind of organization is this? Nothing below can be asked until this is settled, since the branches diverge substantially.

- Government / state — holds formal political authority over a territory or people
- Guild / professional body — organized around a trade, skill, or profession
- Religious organization — the institutional/hierarchical arm of a belief system (as opposed to the belief system itself, which is the separate Religion category — fact-find whether a matching Religion note exists and link rather than duplicate doctrine here)
- Criminal / underground — operates outside or against established law
- Military / martial order — organized around armed force, combat training, or a warrior code
- Mercantile / commercial — organized around trade, production, or commerce
- Social / fraternal / secret society — bound by shared identity, belief, or oath rather than any of the above

If none of these fit, or the faction genuinely spans more than one (a mercantile house that's also become a shadow government, a martial order that's also a religious one), don't force a single gate — note the blend and carry forward whichever branches below are relevant to each aspect.

## Cross-type branches (ask regardless of which gate above was taken)

- What is the faction's stated goal or purpose — and does it have a different true/hidden goal, known only to some members or none at all?
- How is leadership structured, and how does power transfer (inheritance, election, combat, seniority, divine mandate, force)? If the faction has multiple named ranks or titles worth distinguishing, don't just describe the hierarchy in the abstract — for each rank worth naming, capture what it takes to hold it (qualifications/requirements), how someone is appointed or promoted into it, its duties and any perks that come with it, and what would get someone removed from it. Keep this compact per rank (a few lines each, not a full separate interview) unless one specific rank is narratively central enough to warrant real depth. These get written into the faction's own Ranks & Titles section (see Document sections below) rather than spun off as separate notes.
- How does someone join — open membership, invitation, birthright, trial, purchase? What would get someone expelled or killed for leaving?
- What actually gives this faction its power or leverage — wealth, numbers, military force, information/blackmail, magic, political legitimacy, popular support?
- Is the faction internally unified, or are there real fault lines — rival leadership claims, factions-within-the-faction, an old guard versus reformers? (This matters for how much "the faction" can act with one voice on the page.)
- Who are its foreign relations/rivals — allies, enemies, neutral parties, and anyone it directly borders or competes with? (Fact-find first — overlaps with the Connections fragment, but this one is substantial enough to warrant its own document section rather than staying a one-line connection.)

## Government / state branch

- What system of governance — monarchy, republic, oligarchy, theocracy, council, something without a clean real-world analogue?
- What territory or population does it actually control, versus claim to control?
- What makes its authority legitimate in the eyes of those it governs — tradition, force, divine sanction, consent, some mix — and is that legitimacy currently secure or contested?
- Is there a distinct legislative body (who makes law) and judicial body (who interprets/enforces it), or does the same authority hold all of these?

This branch also pulls in the shared Economic & Trade block below, since a state almost always has these questions worth asking.

## Guild / professional branch

- What trade, skill, or profession is it organized around?
- Does it hold a monopoly or licensing power over that trade, and how is that enforced?
- How does training/advancement work — apprenticeship, ranks, examinations?

## Religious organization branch

- What Religion/Deity does it serve or represent, if that's already established in the notes (fact-find first)? If no matching Religion note exists yet, flag that as a likely follow-up rather than inventing doctrine here.
- What's its role relative to belief and practice — does it enforce orthodoxy, merely facilitate worship, or something looser?
- Is it the sole institutional voice of its faith, or one of several competing bodies (sects/denominations) claiming the same religious authority?
- Does membership/clergy status grant any real, mechanical power in the setting (blessings, magic, authority recognized by law), or is its power purely social/institutional?

This branch's document sections run considerably deeper than the others — see Document sections below — since religious organizations tend to need the most distinct kinds of detail (doctrine, cosmology, practice, hierarchy) of any faction type.

## Criminal / underground branch

- What's the actual illicit activity or activities?
- How does it evade or coexist with legitimate authority — bribery, secrecy, being too useful to eliminate, operating somewhere authority doesn't reach?
- Does it claim or contest territory, and against whom?

## Military / martial order branch

- Who or what does it serve — a state, a religion, itself, a code independent of any of those?
- Is there a formal code of conduct or oath, and what happens when it's broken?
- Does it specialize (a particular combat style, terrain, function) or operate as general-purpose force?

## Mercantile / commercial branch

- What does it trade, produce, or control the flow of?
- Does it face real competition, or does it hold something close to a monopoly?
- How does wealth generated actually get used — reinvestment, political influence, luxury, funding something else entirely?

This branch also pulls in the shared Economic & Trade block below.

## Social / fraternal / secret society branch

- How secret is it, actually — unknown to exist, known to exist but membership is secret, open but exclusive?
- What binds members beyond stated purpose — shared class, shared secret, blood oath, shared belief that doesn't rise to a full Religion?
- What's the actual consequence of secrecy being broken, and by whom is it enforced?

## Shared Economic & Trade block (Government/State and Mercantile/Commercial types only)

- What's the economic system (market, planned, guild-controlled, barter, something else)?
- What currency is used, if the setting has one distinct to this faction?
- What are its major exports and imports?
- Is agriculture/industry or trade/transport more central to how it sustains itself?

## Document sections (prose body)

Cross-type, used by every faction regardless of gate: **Structure** (how the hierarchy actually works day to day), **Ranks & Titles** (only if the faction has named positions worth distinguishing — one subsection per rank, each covering qualifications, appointment/promotion, duties, benefits, and grounds for removal, plus any notable current or past holders linked out to Character notes; keep it compact rather than treating each rank as its own full entity), **Public Agenda** (the stated purpose — private/hidden goals belong in prose too, but flagged clearly as not public knowledge), **Public Perception** — absent from WA's fields, which only cover how the faction presents itself, not whether anyone believes it: does the general population (non-members) see this faction as beloved, feared, distrusted, unknown, or something that varies by region/class? Worth asking explicitly, since Public Agenda and Public Perception can diverge sharply and that gap is often narratively useful. **Assets**, **History**, **Foreign Relations**, **Notable Members** (link out to Character notes rather than duplicating their detail here). Include **Disbandment** only if Status marks this faction as historical/dissolved.

Government/State additional: **Demographics & Population**, **Controlled Territories**, **Government System**, **Power Structure**, **Legislative Body**, **Judicial Body**, **Military**, **Official Languages**, **Neighboring Nations**, plus the shared **Economic System / Currency / Exports & Imports / Agriculture & Industry / Trade & Transport** sections, and optionally **Education** and **Infrastructure** if relevant to the story.

Religious organization additional — the deepest section list of any type, use what's relevant rather than forcing all of it: **Mythology & Lore**, **Divine Origins**, **Cosmological Views**, **Tenets of Faith**, **Ethics**, **Worship Practices**, **Priesthood** (clergy structure), **Granted Divine Powers** (only if the setting has gods who mechanically grant real power), **Sects** (internal denominational splits), **Political Influence & Intrigue**.

Guild additional: **Trade & Licensing**.

Criminal additional: **Operations & Territory**.

Military/martial additional: **Code & Specialization**.

Mercantile additional: shares the Government/State Economic block above rather than having its own separate version.

Social/secret additional: **Secrecy & Bonds**.

## Then append `_shared.md`

Narrative function, connections to existing material, tone consistency — in that order, after the relevant branch(es) above are resolved.
