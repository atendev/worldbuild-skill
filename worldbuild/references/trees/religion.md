# Religion / Belief System

No dedicated WA template. This tree uses the religious-organization fields already folded into `organization.html.twig` (Mythology & Lore, Divine Origins, Cosmological Views, Tenets of Faith, Ethics, Worship, Priesthood, Sects). Faction's Religious-organization branch uses the same fields for its own document sections.

**Scope note.** Settle this before drafting an entry. This category is for the belief system itself, meaning doctrine, cosmology, and practice, independent of any one institution. If a religion has exactly one unified institutional voice and no doctrine/institution split worth documenting separately, use a Faction note (Type = Religious organization) and skip a separate Religion note. Do not create redundant notes covering the same ground twice. Religion earns its own note when:

- multiple competing Faction entries (sects, rival churches) share one underlying belief system,
- the belief system predates or has outlived any institution built around it, or
- the belief is decentralized (folk religion, philosophical tradition) and has no single institutional body to hold the doctrine.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type** (monotheistic, polytheistic, animist, philosophical/non-theistic), **Founder/origin** (relation to Character or Deity, fact-find first), **Primary institutional body** (relation to a Faction note, if one exists and is the dominant voice. Fact-find, and leave blank if the religion is decentralized).

## Document sections (prose body)

**Mythology & Lore**, **Cosmological Views**, **Afterlife Beliefs**. WA's religious fields leave out Afterlife Beliefs entirely, though what happens after death and how that shapes behavior in life is the defining question of most real-world religions. Ask about it directly. Do not assume Cosmological Views covers it, since that section tends to describe the structure of the universe, not what happens to a person within it. **Tenets of Faith**, **Ethics**, **Worship Practices**, **Symbols & Iconography** (distinct from any one Deity's personal symbol in Character's Divine section. This is the religion's own recognizable imagery, whichever specific god it centers on, if any), **Divine Origins** (if the tradition holds specific gods responsible for its founding, with a relation to Deity/Character notes), **Sects & Denominations** (link to the Faction notes for each rather than describing their institutional differences here), **History & Spread**, **Relationship to Other Religions** (syncretism, rivalry, tolerance, persecution).
