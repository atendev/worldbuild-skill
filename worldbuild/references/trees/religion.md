# Religion / Belief System

No dedicated WA template — draws on the religious-organization fields already folded into `organization.html.twig` (Mythology & Lore, Divine Origins, Cosmological Views, Tenets of Faith, Ethics, Worship, Priesthood, Sects), which is also what Faction's Religious-organization branch already uses for its own document sections.

**Scope note, worth getting right before drafting an entry:** this category is for the belief system itself — doctrine, cosmology, practice — independent of any one institution. If a religion has exactly one unified institutional voice with no meaningful doctrine/institution split worth documenting separately, it's fine to just use a Faction note (Type = Religious organization) and skip a separate Religion note entirely — don't create redundant notes covering the same ground twice. Religion earns its own note when: multiple competing Faction entries (sects, rival churches) share one underlying belief system, the belief system meaningfully predates or has outlived any institution built around it, or the belief is genuinely decentralized (folk religion, philosophical tradition) with no single institutional body to hang the doctrine on.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type** (monotheistic, polytheistic, animist, philosophical/non-theistic), **Founder/origin** (relation to Character or Deity, fact-find first), **Primary institutional body** (relation to a Faction note, if one exists and is the dominant voice — fact-find; leave blank if genuinely decentralized).

## Document sections (prose body)

**Mythology & Lore**, **Cosmological Views**, **Afterlife Beliefs** — absent from WA's religious fields entirely, despite being close to the defining question of most real-world religions (what happens after death, and how that shapes behavior in life); worth asking directly rather than assuming it's covered by Cosmological Views, which tends to focus on the structure of the universe rather than what happens to a person within it. **Tenets of Faith**, **Ethics**, **Worship Practices**, **Symbols & Iconography** (distinct from any one Deity's personal symbol in Character's Divine section — this is the religion's own recognizable imagery, independent of which specific god it centers on, if any), **Divine Origins** (if the tradition holds specific gods responsible for its founding — relation to Deity/Character notes), **Sects & Denominations** (link to the relevant Faction notes representing each, rather than describing their institutional differences here), **History & Spread**, **Relationship to Other Religions** (syncretism, rivalry, tolerance, persecution).
