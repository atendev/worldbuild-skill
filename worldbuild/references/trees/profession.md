# Profession / Occupation

WA source: `profession.html.twig`.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type** (trade, craft, service, profession requiring licensure), **Legality**, **Social status**, **Typical workplace**, **Pay/Demand** (a short descriptor rather than hard numbers unless the setting's economy warrants precision).

## Document sections (prose body)

**Purpose** (what this profession actually does for society), **Qualifications** (how one enters this line of work — training, apprenticeship, licensing — relation to a Faction/Guild if one governs entry), **Tools & Materials**, **Benefits & Hazards**, **Services Provided**, **Related Ranks/Titles** (relation, if this profession has an internal hierarchy worth noting — otherwise this belongs in a Faction's own Ranks & Titles section rather than being duplicated here).
