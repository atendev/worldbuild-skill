# Profession / Occupation

WA source: `profession.html.twig`.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type** (trade, craft, service, or profession requiring licensure), **Legality**, **Social status**, **Typical workplace**, **Pay/Demand** (a short descriptor rather than hard numbers unless the setting's economy needs precision).

## Document sections (prose body)

**Purpose** (what this profession does for society), **Qualifications** (how one enters this line of work, such as training, apprenticeship, or licensing, and the relation to a Faction/Guild if one governs entry), **Tools & Materials**, **Benefits & Hazards**, **Services Provided**, **Related Ranks/Titles** (a relation, if this profession has an internal hierarchy worth noting. Otherwise it belongs in a Faction's own Ranks & Titles section, not duplicated here).
