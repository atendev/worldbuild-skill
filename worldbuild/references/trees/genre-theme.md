# Genre & Theme Template (meta)

This category formalizes what SKILL.md Step 2a already does ad hoc when drafting a brand-new tree. It does so deliberately, for a whole genre at once, rather than one category at a time as gaps are hit.

## Questions

- Is this abstracting from the user's own existing notes, finding recurring patterns already present in what they have written, or from outside genre research, meaning tropes and structure common to the genre generally, independent of what has been written so far? Both are valid and they produce different guidance. The former captures the user's own instincts. The latter captures reader expectations the user may not have matched yet.
- What should the output inform? It could be new tree branches for a specific category, such as a genre-specific twist on what Location or Character should ask. It could also be broader tone or trope guidance that is not tied to one category.

## Synthesis approach

If working from existing notes, scan several examples of the relevant category and look for recurring patterns not yet captured in that category's own tree file. Propose them as additions, following Step 2a's process, rather than as a separate document. If working from outside research, compile trope and structure findings into a reference note the user can point future interviews at, as a Style/tone guide is pointed at in Step 6.
