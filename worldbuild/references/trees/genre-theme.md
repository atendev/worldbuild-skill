# Genre & Theme Template (meta)

This category formalizes what SKILL.md Step 2a already does ad hoc when drafting a brand-new tree — doing it deliberately, for a whole genre at once, rather than one category at a time as gaps are hit.

## Questions

- Is this abstracting from the user's own existing notes (finding recurring patterns already present in what they've written) or from outside genre research (tropes/structure common to the genre generally, independent of what's been written so far)? Both are valid; they produce different kinds of guidance — the former captures the user's own instincts, the latter captures reader expectations they may not have consciously matched yet.
- What's the output actually meant to inform — new tree branches for a specific category (a genre-specific twist on what Location or Character should ask), or something broader like tone/trope guidance that isn't tied to one category?

## Synthesis approach

If working from existing notes: scan several examples of the relevant category, look for recurring patterns not yet captured in that category's own tree file, and propose them as additions (following Step 2a's process) rather than a wholly separate document. If working from outside research: compile trope/structure findings into a reference note the user can point future interviews at, similar to how a Style/tone guide gets pointed at in Step 6.
