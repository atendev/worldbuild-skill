# Document / Text

WA source: `document.html.twig`. Distinct from Artifact by being content-centric (what it says) rather than object-centric (what it physically is) — a treaty's text matters more than its parchment, where an Artifact's physical properties are the point.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type** (treaty, law, letter, book, decree, prophecy), **Author(s)** (relation to Character), **Signatories** — characters and organizations, if applicable (relation lists), **Authoring/ratification/expiration dates**, **Legality**, **Publicity** (public record, secret, restricted access).

## Document sections (prose body)

**Purpose**, **Background** (what circumstances produced it), **Clauses/Terms/Contents** (a summary — full verbatim reproduction only if genuinely useful and short; otherwise summarize), **Public Reaction**, **Legacy** (what it changed or continues to affect), **Caveats/Loopholes** (if narratively relevant), **Referenced By** (link to other notes/events that cite or depend on this document).
