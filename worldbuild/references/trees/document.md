# Document / Text

WA source: `document.html.twig`. Document differs from Artifact because it is about content, what the text says, rather than the object and what it physically is. A treaty's text matters more than its parchment, while an Artifact's physical properties are the point.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus the following. **Type** is one of treaty, law, letter, book, decree, or prophecy. **Author(s)** is a relation to Character. **Signatories** are characters and organizations, if applicable, as relation lists. **Authoring/ratification/expiration dates**. **Legality**. **Publicity** is public record, secret, or restricted access.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Purpose**. **Background**, meaning the circumstances that produced it. **Clauses/Terms/Contents**, a summary. Reproduce the full text verbatim only if it is useful and short, and otherwise summarize. **Public Reaction**. **Legacy**, meaning what it changed or continues to affect. **Caveats/Loopholes**, if narratively relevant. **Referenced By**, with links to other notes and events that cite or depend on this document.
