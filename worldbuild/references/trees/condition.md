# Condition / Affliction

WA source: `condition.html.twig`. Covers disease, curses, and magical afflictions under one flexible shape.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type** (disease, curse, genetic/hereditary, magical, environmental), **Rarity**, **Affected species** (relation, fact-find first), **Transmission** (if contagious — vector type), **Prognosis** (a short descriptor).

## Document sections (prose body)

**Causes & Origin**, **Symptoms**, **Transmission** (only if contagious — omit entirely for a non-communicable condition like a curse tied to one individual or a hereditary trait), **Treatment & Prevention**, **Prognosis & Sequela** (long-term/lasting effects even after the acute condition resolves), **Epidemiology** (how widespread it is, affected demographics — only relevant at population scale, skip for a curse affecting one named character), **Cultural Reception** (how society regards those affected — stigma, superstition, medical understanding or lack thereof), **History**.
