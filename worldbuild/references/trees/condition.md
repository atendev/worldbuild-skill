# Condition / Affliction

WA source: `condition.html.twig`. Covers disease, curses, and magical afflictions under one flexible shape.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type**, either disease, curse, genetic/hereditary, magical, or environmental; **Rarity**; **Affected species**, a relation, fact-find first; **Transmission**, if contagious, giving how it spreads; **Prognosis**, a short descriptor.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Causes & Origin**; **Symptoms**; **Transmission**, only if contagious, so omit it entirely for a non-communicable condition such as a curse tied to one individual or a hereditary trait; **Treatment & Prevention**; **Prognosis & Sequela**, the long-term effects that last after the acute condition resolves; **Epidemiology**, how widespread it is and which demographics are affected, relevant only at population scale, so skip it for a curse affecting one named character; **Cultural Reception**, how society regards those affected, including stigma, superstition, and how well the condition is medically understood; **History**.
