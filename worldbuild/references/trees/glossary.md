# Glossary (compilation)

Shallow scope/audience/format tree — see SKILL.md Step 4, compilation mode.

## Questions

- Scope — every named term/proper noun in the notes, or a curated subset (e.g. only invented vocabulary a reader would need defined, not every character name)?
- Format — alphabetical, categorized (people/places/things), or both?
- Does the setting have its own Language notes with vocabulary sections already? If so, fact-find and pull from those rather than re-deriving definitions from scratch.

## Synthesis approach

Compile terms and short definitions from across the notes. A glossary entry should be genuinely short — a sentence or two — with a pointer to the full note for anything that needs more.
