# Glossary (compilation)

This is a shallow scope, audience, and format tree. See SKILL.md Step 4, compilation mode.

## Questions

- Scope. Should it cover every named term and proper noun in the notes, or a curated subset, such as only the invented vocabulary a reader would need defined and not every character name?
- Format. Should it be alphabetical, categorized into people, places, and things, or both?
- Does the setting have its own Language notes with vocabulary sections already? If so, fact-find and pull from those rather than re-deriving definitions from scratch.

## Synthesis approach

Compile terms and short definitions from across the notes. A glossary entry should be short, a sentence or two, with a pointer to the full note for anything that needs more.
