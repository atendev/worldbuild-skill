# Relationship Map (compilation)

Shallow scope/audience/format tree — see SKILL.md Step 4, compilation mode. Compiles across Character notes' Social sections (Contacts & Relations, Family Ties).

## Questions

- Scope — the full cast, or one specific character's immediate network?
- Format — a written summary, or something structured enough to describe as a diagram (the user may want this handed to the Visualizer or a diagramming tool rather than rendered as prose — ask rather than assuming).

## Synthesis approach

Compile from each relevant Character note's Contacts & Relations and Family Ties sections. Flag one-sided relationships found during compilation (A's note claims a bond B's note never mentions) as a continuity gap worth the user's attention, not something to silently resolve one way.
