# Relationship Map (compilation)

Shallow scope/audience/format tree. See SKILL.md Step 4, compilation mode. Compiles across Character notes' Social sections (Contacts & Relations, Family Ties).

## Questions

- Scope: the full cast, or one specific character's immediate network?
- Format: a written summary, or something structured enough to describe as a diagram? The user may want this handed to the Visualizer or a diagramming tool instead of written as prose. Ask rather than assume.

## Synthesis approach

Compile from each relevant Character note's Contacts & Relations and Family Ties sections. Flag one-sided relationships found during compilation, where A's note claims a bond that B's note never mentions, as a continuity gap for the user's attention. Do not silently resolve them one way.
