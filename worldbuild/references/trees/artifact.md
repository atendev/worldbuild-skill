# Artifact / Object

WA source: `item.html.twig`.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type**, **Rarity**, **Value/Price**, **Weight**, **Dimensions**, **Manufacturer** (relation to Faction or Character), **Current holder** (relation to Character, fact-find first), **Current location** (relation to Location), **Condition**.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Significance** (why this object matters narratively — this is the section most worth not skimping on; a rich infobox with a thin Significance section produces a prop, not an artifact), **Manufacturing Process**, **Raw Materials** (relation to Material notes), **Mechanics** (how it's used/operated, especially if it does something beyond being decorative), **History/Provenance** (who's owned it, notable events it's been part of), **Datasheet** (only if the setting uses mechanical item stats).
