# Artifact / Object

WA source: `item.html.twig`.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Type**; **Rarity**; **Value/Price**; **Weight**; **Dimensions**; **Manufacturer**, a relation to Faction or Character; **Current holder**, a relation to Character, fact-find first; **Current location**, a relation to Location; **Condition**.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Significance**, why this object matters narratively. Do not skimp on this section, because a rich infobox with a thin Significance section produces a prop, not an artifact. Then **Manufacturing Process**; **Raw Materials**, a relation to Material notes; **Mechanics**, how it is used or operated, especially if it does something beyond being decorative; **History/Provenance**, who has owned it and notable events it has been part of; **Datasheet**, only if the setting uses mechanical item stats.
