**Execution** (how it's actually performed, step by step if that level of detail matters to the story), **Participants** (who takes part, and in what role), **Components** (materials, objects, or preparations required — relation to Artifact/Material notes if applicable), **Observance** (public celebration versus private/restricted practice), **History/Origin**.
