# Holiday / Custom / Ritual

WA source: `ritual.html.twig`.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Observed by**, a relation to Culture or Faction, fact-find first; **Location**, a relation, if tied to a specific place; **Frequency/timing**, such as annual, once-in-a-lifetime, or triggered by a specific circumstance.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Execution**, how it is performed, step by step if that level of detail matters to the story; **Participants**, who takes part and in what role; **Components**, the materials, objects, or preparations required, with relations to Artifact or Material notes if applicable; **Observance**, public celebration versus private or restricted practice; **History/Origin**.
