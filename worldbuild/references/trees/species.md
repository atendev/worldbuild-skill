# Species / Race / Ancestry

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Scientific/trinomial name** (if the setting uses formal taxonomy), **Geographic origin**, **Lifespan**, **Average height/length/weight**, **Diet**, **Extinct?**, **Domesticated?** (if applicable to a non-sapient species), **Technological level** (sapient species only).

## Toggle — Sapient?

Does this species have culture, language, and individual agency worth documenting (a people), or is it non-sapient (an animal, plant, or beast)? This gates most of the branches below rather than a hard type split — a sapient species uses the full tree; a non-sapient one skips straight to biology/ecology.

## Branches (sapient species only)

- Naming traditions — how are individuals named, and does it vary by gender/region/clan?
- Social structure — family units, clan/tribal organization, or something else; how does it interact with any Faction/Culture notes that already exist for this species (fact-find first)?
- What ideals does this culture hold around beauty, courtship, relationships, and coming-of-age — and do these vary enough across the species that separate Culture/ethnicity notes make more sense than one blanket answer here?
- Taboos and etiquette worth knowing before writing a scene involving this species.
- Do multiple distinct ethnicities/cultures exist within this species? If so, this note should stay at the species-wide level (biology, shared traits, broad tendencies) and defer culture-specific detail to separate Culture notes rather than trying to cover both here.

## Document sections (prose body)

Universal: **Anatomy & Physical Traits**, **Sensory Capabilities** — absent from WA's `species` fields, but essential for writing convincingly from this species' point of view: do they perceive the world differently than a human baseline (echolocation, infrared vision, no sense of smell, entirely different sensory priorities)? Skip if the species is sensorily unremarkable/human-equivalent — don't force an answer where "same as human" is the honest one. **Ecology & Habitat** (diet, biocycle, growth rate, symbiotic relationships), **Genetics & Ancestry** (related/descendant species — link rather than duplicate), **History**, **Interspecies Relations**.

Sapient-only additional: **Culture & Customs**, **Naming Traditions**, **Social Structure**, **Etiquette & Taboos**, **Historical Figures**, **Myths & Legends** (link to Myth notes), **Languages** (relation), **Ethnicities** (relation to Culture notes, if the species has sub-cultures), **Major Organizations** (relation to Faction).

Non-sapient additional (only if domesticated or otherwise used by sapient peoples): **Domestication & Uses**, **Conservation Status**.
