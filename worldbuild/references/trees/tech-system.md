# Technology / Tech System

WA source: `technology.html.twig`. It is short compared to most WA templates, so technology entries in most settings should stay lean unless the technology works like a magic system. In that case, use that category's Cost/Access/Limitation questions instead.

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Complexity** (a short descriptor of how sophisticated or rare the tech is), **Access** (who has it: universal, restricted, or one faction's monopoly), **Inventor** (relation to Character, if known), **Discovery/invention date**.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Utility** (what it does and why it matters), **Manufacturing** (how it is made and what that implies about scarcity), **Materials Required** (relation to Material notes), **Related Items** (relation to Artifact notes, meaning specific objects that use this technology).

If this technology works like a magic system in the story, with real costs, limits, and social implications instead of being background detail, consider whether the Magic System tree fits better, or whether to add its Costs & Limitations branch here.
