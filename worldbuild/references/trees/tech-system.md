# Technology / Tech System

WA source: `technology.html.twig` — notably short compared to most WA templates, which suggests technology entries in most settings stay fairly lean unless they function like a magic system (in which case, draw more heavily on that category's Cost/Access/Limitation framing instead).

## Infobox fields

Shared Name/Status plus: **Complexity** (a short descriptor of how sophisticated/rare the tech is), **Access** (who has it — universal, restricted, one faction's monopoly), **Inventor** (relation to Character, if known), **Discovery/invention date**.

## Document sections (prose body)

**Utility** (what it does and why it matters), **Manufacturing** (how it's made, what that implies about scarcity), **Materials Required** (relation to Material notes), **Related Items** (relation to Artifact notes — specific objects that use this technology).

If this technology functions like a magic system in the story (has real costs, limits, and social implications rather than just being background-world-building), consider whether it's better served by the Magic System tree instead, or by borrowing its Costs & Limitations branch as an addition here.
